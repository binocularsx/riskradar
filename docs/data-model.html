DEFINITIONS AND DECISIONS
FEATURE SPEC 1.3.0 · 15 SEPTEMBER 2026

The four records, and which can change

Transactionone payment attempt · immutable
scored
Decisionone per transaction · immutable
if actionable
Alertan observation · immutable
correlated
Casethe investigation · the only mutable record

Decision stores the probability, risk level, the rules' signals, the full feature snapshot and the versions of model, ruleset, thresholds and feature spec, so any decision can be reproduced months later. Case holds many alerts: a new alert joins an open case with the same subject_token (the customer, not the account) within 24 hours; otherwise it opens a new case. Since spec 1.2.0 a payment is one type of event (D72): logins, device binding, credential and SIM changes, payee additions and credits share one envelope, are tokenised at the same boundary, and are read by measurements; only payments and credits are decided.

Case states

OPEN → UNDER_REVIEW → CONFIRMED_FRAUD | FALSE_POSITIVE | INCONCLUSIVE → CLOSED
UNDER_REVIEW → ESCALATED → INFOSEC | FRAUD_OPS → back to review, or CLOSED

Inconclusive is mandatory: a forced yes/no under time pressure poisons the labels. Outcomes are the only real training labels this system will ever produce.

Decisions that shape the data

D9bTwo timestampsLate batches scored on arrival time fake a burst; keeping one timestamp loses the truth for good.
—Integer koboFloating-point money is finance software's commonest defect; every total inherits it.
D19Two identity levelsCases follow the customer, so one takeover across three accounts is one case; baselines follow the account.
D9c–dOne-way tokensHashed with a secret pepper at intake; no reversal table. The token travels; the receiving bank looks it up.
D21Attempts, not successesWithout refusals, card testing is invisible and reversals count money twice.
D22Stamp, never joinJoining today's account table would leak the future into training and change old decisions.
D15One measurement packageTraining and live call the same functions; a test proves identical numbers.
D25, D77–78Twelve, then twenty-twoTwelve cut from fifteen to pay for the identity work; five event and five credit measurements added once their data existed.
D78bThe model reads seventeenThe five receiving-side measurements made unseen takeover worse (0.991 to 0.857); rules read them, every decision stores all.
D64−1 and the capThe old marker 999 read as a trusted old payee and helped hide card-testing fraud.
D10fNo borrowed scoresNever train on another party's "risk", "score", "rate" or "fraud" column unless its code can be read.

−1 means "does not apply" (no destination, no date) and sits outside every real range. Ratios are capped at 100. History looked at: account 30 days, customer 30 days, destinations 90 days, events 72 hours, credits 30 days. Outgoing history reads OUTBOUND only, so no older measurement changed meaning when credits arrived. The spec version is stored on every decision.
